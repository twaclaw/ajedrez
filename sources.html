Sources

Where each game’s moves were read from. Nothing here is a reconstruction: the moves were taken from a published record, replayed on a board to check that every one of them is legal, and cross-checked against the result the source reports. Where two sources disagreed, both are listed and the chapter says so.

The notes are written for this book unless the note says whose judgement it is reporting. Where a note carries a name, that name is the person the assessment belongs to, and the source it was read in is listed with the game.
